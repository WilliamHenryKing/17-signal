# SIGNAL

Independent Chamber Group concept 02, 1 October 2026. Read root CHAMBER-DEMO-PLAN.md. Common Ground is already verified and published; Signal is built second, solo.

Art direction: a confident typographic broadcast. Midnight #111c2a, bright bone #f2f0e5, acid lime #d8f36a, steel blue #647d96. Barlow Condensed is large, authoritative and tight; Manrope handles quiet explanatory type. A woven signal graphic is made from fine parallel SVG curves, not a bitmap effect. Small sector tags and registration rules give the scale context. One carefully cropped conference photograph anchors the abstract pattern in human exchange.

Opening: a five-line signal draws toward a shared point while MAKE YOUR / VOICE / MATTER. rises in staggered masks. VOICE has an offset stroke echo that converges into its solid form. A compact image window opens, the labels resolve, and the full scene holds within four seconds. Scroll expands the clear lime statement into view and continues the signal/line language through the homepage. No loading gate or infinite background effects.

Body: lime manifesto → interactive five-sector capability console (horizontal number selector and large active panel) → editorial notes in a contrasting asymmetric layout → a compact local conversation planner → huge outlined closing wordmark. No reused circular graphic or ivory editorial hero from 16. Native scrolling; shorter phone sequence and a stacked poster composition.

All identity/copy/artwork is provisional and explicitly independent. No invented credentials, client case studies or contact addresses. Original vector graphics; local verified photographs and OFL fonts. Hero HTML/CSS/JS is independently integrable and prerendered.
