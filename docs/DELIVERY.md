## How the brief is answered

- **Creative concept:** all five disciplines — public affairs, policy, communications, membership and events — contribute to each distinct hero and continue through its homepage. Original SVG artwork and logo treatments establish the two provisional identities.
- **Motion and transitions:** short GSAP arrivals combine type, graphic construction and image framing, then settle. Scroll choreography continues the same shapes and typographic language into the content. Replay, motion-off and live operating-system reduced-motion changes work.
- **A complete experience:** navigation, five-discipline explorers, full editorial dialogs and local brief preparation have working interactions. Copy/download tools do not send information or pretend to contact a business.
- **Responsive delivery:** actual Chrome interaction and visual inspection cover six desktop, tablet, phone and landscape viewports, including 320 px width. Focus, mobile menus, Escape, validation and keyboard selection were exercised.
- **Fast loading:** no video or WebGL payload, runtime CDN, analytics or backend. Local WOFF2 fonts and compressed WebP photographs; secondary imagery loads lazily. Fresh-context live resource transfer was approximately 318 KB and 352 KB respectively, excluding the HTML response. These are observations on this host/network, not physical-phone speed guarantees.
- **Integration:** a framework-free hero build accompanies each complete React homepage. Scoped styles and explicit timeline setup/cleanup are documented. Prerendered content remains readable without JavaScript.
